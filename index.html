About Me

I'm Rajat Jhawar, a data analyst and data engineer who builds the pipelines and the reporting on top of them. U.S. Housing Market Trends and Forecasting with Tableau and Python

Company Performance Report with Power BI

Found that 2023 sold 3.2% more units but earned 3.8% less, with China alone losing $405K of gross profit.
